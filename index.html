How Transactions Work

Add your income and expenses to keep track of your spending. Each transaction includes a type, description, category, and amount.

Your transactions are used to calculate your total income, total expenses, and current balance.

Example: If you receive $500 in income and spend $150 on groceries, your current balance would be $350.
