Review your income, expenses, balance, remaining budget, and spending by category to better understand your finances.

Example: If you earn $2,000 and spend $1,200, your balance is $800. The category breakdown can help you see whether most of your spending goes toward food, housing, transportation, school, or other expenses.

How the Dashboard Works

The analytics page uses the transactions you add to the Student Budget Tracker to give you a clearer picture of your finances.

Your income and expenses are totaled to calculate your current balance. Your spending is also organized into categories so you can see where your money is going.

If you set a monthly budget, the Budget Status section shows how much of your budget you have used and how much remains.
